The idea behind SkillBridge

Learning should be a two-way street.

Traditional learning often focuses on one person teaching and another learning. SkillBridge makes knowledge exchange mutual. Share your strengths, discover new opportunities and learn from one another.

Simple by design

How SkillBridge Works

Three simple steps to start exchanging knowledge.

STEP 01

Share Your Skills

Add the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn. Build your learning profile around your interests.

STEP 02

Find Your Match

Discover people who have the knowledge you're looking for and identify opportunities for a meaningful skill exchange.

STEP 03

Exchange Knowledge

Send an exchange request, connect with another learner and start growing together.
